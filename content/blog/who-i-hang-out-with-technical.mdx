## Overview

From the user's perspective, the user sees this set of widgets on their home screen,
with the main hangout widget changing each day.
The hangout widget has the most recent days on the outside with the older days on the inside of the radial plot.
Each circle on a given day represents a single person that I hung out with on that day,
and the widget updates each day and highlights four random people on whom I hung out with in the past month.
