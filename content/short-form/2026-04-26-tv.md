The lined display of the old television is a clear indication that it is on but so is the faint hiss of the gun buried within. Those who meet this TV for the first time are quick to exclaim that this TV hums and they continue their day unbothered by this fact - for a TV that hums is one that sings and one that sings is one that's on. Some though have watched this TV and have grown quite familiar with it being on. Their eyes watch their friends dance across the screen but their ears hear no song. Ask if it hums and they occasionally stumble into the correct answer but do not be fooled, no such hum is in their ear. They have already forgotten it. Do not let them tell you the nature of this TV though they insist they have seen it the longest.
