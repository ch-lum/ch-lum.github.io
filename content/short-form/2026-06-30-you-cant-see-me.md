title: you cant see me
date: 2026-06-30

922 pm 18 minutes and counting under the bed dust 
collecting settling being found long forgotten 
ball three rubber bands old girlfriends bobby pin and 
myself playing hide and seek

dark room not black cant see but light 
spills from door legs walk by many spots 
to hide to check to find only one me and 
i think im good

second hand walks ticks step step steps 
into the room light still off though heightened 
hearing fades away hum air conditioning off or 
stress because someones here

legs im unsure where they are body 
numb under bed foot might stick out trip looker 
id laugh but be found so instead 
i try to tuck

nothing feels right still cant see or feel or hear 
whats going on the game ill keep playing where are 
my arms is my head under i think i can be seen they 
can see if i move so i lie still

this is fun but if i stay im seen i move im seen i quit 
im seen what is the point im seen ask for help 
im seen live im seen die im seen

all to say i love hide and seek
